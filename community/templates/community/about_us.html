WHO WE ARE

HISTORY

Founded with a love for music and a passion for vinyl, Vinyl Online has been bringing the magic of records to collectors and casual listeners alike. Our journey began with a small community of enthusiasts sharing rare finds and classic albums, and today we’re proud to serve music lovers across the country with an ever-growing catalog of new and vintage vinyl.

We believe that music is meant to be experienced fully, and there’s nothing quite like the warm, rich sound of a record spinning on a turntable. At Vinyl Online, we’re more than a store - we’re a community of listeners who celebrate the artistry, history, and joy of music. Whether you’re hunting for that hard-to-find pressing or discovering your first record, we’re here to make every listening experience unforgettable.

HOW WE HELP ARTISTS

We’re committed to helping artists reach their audience in a meaningful and sustainable way. By offering a dedicated platform for independent and established musicians alike, we provide artists with the tools to sell their music directly to fans. Every record sold supports the creators behind the music, helping them continue to produce and share their art without losing touch with the community that loves it.

Beyond sales, we help artists grow their visibility and connect with a passionate audience of vinyl collectors. We showcase new releases, limited editions, and exclusive pressings, giving artists the opportunity to stand out and engage with listeners in a way that digital platforms can’t replicate. Our focus on quality, authenticity, and community ensures that artists receive the recognition and support they deserve while fans enjoy music the way it was meant to be experienced.

HOW WE HELP YOU

We put our customers first, ensuring that every purchase and interaction is smooth and enjoyable. Whether you’re browsing for new releases or tracking down a rare pressing, our team is always available to assist. We provide responsive support through multiple channels, including email, phone, and our online support ticket system, so you can get the help you need quickly and efficiently.

We understand that collecting vinyl is about more than just buying music - it’s about the experience. That’s why our customer service goes beyond transactions, offering guidance on orders, shipping, and product care. Every ticket you submit is handled personally, and our support team works diligently to resolve issues, answer questions, and make sure your Vinyl Online experience is seamless from start to finish.
